# Brief 03 · Attention from scratch

- File: `pages/03-attention.html` · Part I · Foundations · P0 · ~50 min · body 4,500–7,000 words
- Prereqs: 01. Drill module name: `attention`

## Purpose
The reader has used attention in an encoder (jet tagging), so skip the "what is attention"
hand-waving and be rigorous and LLM-specific: exact shapes, the scaling argument, causal
masking, multi-head mechanics, numerics, and the quadratic cost with real numbers. Interviewers
routinely ask candidates to implement causal multi-head attention on a whiteboard.

## Must cover
1. **Scaled dot-product attention** with shapes: \(Q \in \R^{T \times d_h}\), \(K \in \R^{S
   \times d_h}\), \(V \in \R^{S \times d_v}\); attention as a soft dictionary lookup /
   kernel smoother. Chinese: 注意力机制, 自注意力.
2. **Why \(1/\sqrt{d_h}\)**: the variance argument (i.i.d. unit-variance components give
   \(\mathrm{Var}(q \cdot k) = d_h\)), and what goes wrong without it (softmax saturation,
   near-one-hot weights, vanishing gradients). Show the softmax Jacobian and why saturation
   kills gradients. Mention that the scale can be folded into \(W^Q\) (Shazeer 2019 note).
3. **Softmax properties**: shift invariance, the numerically stable max-subtraction,
   temperature, and why the output is a convex combination of values.
4. **Causal masking** (因果掩码): the mask matrix, applying \(-\infty\) before softmax, the
   NaN pitfall for fully masked rows, and why training on all positions in parallel is exact
   under the mask. The end-aligned mask for T queries over S keys when a cache is present
   (link to page 06/07).
5. **Multi-head attention** (多头注意力): split \(d\) into \(n_h\) heads of \(d_h = d/n_h\),
   reshape/transpose mechanics \((B, T, d) \to (B, n_h, T, d_h)\), concat and \(W^O\). Why
   multiple heads (different subspaces/patterns at the same FLOPs as one wide head); parameter
   count \(4d^2\) (without GQA). The low-rank bottleneck argument (Bhojanapalli et al. 2020)
   briefly.
6. **Permutation equivariance**: self-attention without positions is permutation-equivariant
   (bridge: this is exactly why it suited the jet tagger's particle sets), so language models
   need positional information: hand-off to page 05.
7. **Cost**: FLOPs for \(QK^\top\) and \(AV\): \(4 T^2 d\) per layer per sequence
   (forward, with multiply-add = 2 FLOPs); memory for materialized scores
   \(B \cdot n_h \cdot T^2\) elements. Worked numbers at Llama-3-8B's shape (d = 4096,
   32 heads) for T = 8K and 128K, compared with the per-layer linear-layer FLOPs, to show where
   attention starts dominating. Point to FlashAttention (page 16) for how the \(T^2\) memory is
   avoided without changing the math.
8. **Self- vs cross-attention**, briefly (encoder-decoder, multimodal).
9. **What heads learn** (brief, interview-relevant): induction heads (Olsson et al. 2022) as
   the mechanism behind in-context copying; attention sinks (a lot of attention mass on the
   first token; Xiao et al. 2023) with a forward link to pages 10/11. Keep it short.

## Required worked numbers
- Variance of \(q \cdot k\) for \(d_h = 128\) with and without scaling; resulting typical
  logit magnitudes.
- Attention-score FLOPs and naive score memory (bf16) per layer at T = 8,192 and 131,072 for
  Llama-3-8B shape; compare to the \(\approx 2 \cdot\) (params per layer) FLOPs per token of the
  linear layers (per-layer params: attention 41.9M + MLP 176.2M, from page 04's count).

## Required figures (2–3)
- Shapes flow of multi-head attention: (B,T,d) → Q,K,V → heads → scores (T×T) → mask →
  softmax → weighted V → concat → W^O.
- The causal mask matrix, including the end-aligned case (T new queries, S cached keys).

## Required interview questions (12–16 total; include these)
- Why do we divide by \(\sqrt{d_h}\)? What happens if we don't?
- Implement causal multi-head attention (answer: a short correct PyTorch implementation).
- What is the time and memory complexity of attention? Where is the bottleneck at 128K tokens?
- Why multiple heads rather than one head of width d? Same parameter count?
- Why is softmax attention permutation-equivariant, and what does that imply?
- A row of your attention mask is fully masked and your loss becomes NaN. Why, and how do you fix it?
- Why can we train on all T positions in parallel but must generate one token at a time?

  `F.scaled_dot_product_attention`.
