# Brief 05 · Positional encoding & RoPE

- File: `pages/05-positional-encoding.html` · Part I · Foundations · P0 · ~55 min · body 5,000–7,500 words
- Prereqs: 03. Drill module name: `rope`

## Purpose
RoPE is used by nearly every current LLM, underlies long-context extension (page 10), and
explains MLA's decoupled design (page 07). The reader must be able to derive RoPE's relative
property on a whiteboard, implement it in both common layouts, and compare it with the
alternatives. Their jet tagger was deliberately permutation-invariant; this page is about
breaking that symmetry correctly.

## Must cover
1. **Why position information is needed**: self-attention is permutation-equivariant (link
   03); a causal LM is *not* fully position-blind (the mask leaks order), which is why NoPE
   can work at all. Bridge callout to the jet tagger. Chinese: 位置编码, 旋转位置编码.
2. **Absolute encodings**: sinusoidal (Vaswani 2017: formulas, wavelengths as a geometric
   progression from \(2\pi\) to \(10000 \cdot 2\pi\), the linear-offset property) and learned
   absolute tables (BERT, GPT-2) with their hard length limit.
3. **Relative encodings**: Shaw et al. 2018 (learned relative embeddings in attention);
   T5's bucketed relative position bias; **ALiBi** (Press et al. 2021: fixed per-head linear
   penalty \(-m_h |t-j|\), geometric slopes, the extrapolation claim and its limits).
4. **RoPE (Su et al. 2021)**, the centerpiece:
   - Rotate each 2-D pair of q and k by angle \(t\,\theta_i\), \(\theta_i = b^{-2i/d_h}\).
   - Complex-number view: \(\tilde q_t = q_t e^{i t \theta}\); prove
     \(\langle \tilde q_t, \tilde k_j \rangle\) depends only on \(t - j\), in both the complex
     and the real block-rotation form (\(R_t^\top R_j = R_{j-t}\)).
   - Applied to q and k only, never v, and after the projections; why.
   - Frequencies and wavelengths: \(\lambda_i = 2\pi b^{2i/d_h}\); fast dimensions encode local
     position, slow ones barely rotate within the training context; the "long-term decay"
     property claimed in the paper (state it carefully and note it is about an upper bound).
   - The **base** \(b\): 10,000 (original, DeepSeek-V3), 500,000 (Llama 3), 1,000,000 or more
     (verify Qwen versions); what raising the base does (lower frequencies, more positions
     before wrap-around), setting up page 10.
   - **Implementation layouts**: interleaved pairs (original paper, GPT-J, DeepSeek reference
     code uses a complex view) vs split halves / `rotate_half` (GPT-NeoX, Hugging Face Llama).
     They are equivalent up to a fixed permutation of q/k dimensions, which can be absorbed into
     \(W^Q, W^K\); mixing them up silently breaks a converted checkpoint. Show both in code.
   - Cost: cheap elementwise ops, cos/sin cache.
   - **Partial RoPE** (rotate only a fraction of dims, GPT-NeoX `rotary_pct`) and
     **decoupled RoPE** in MLA (link 07).
5. **NoPE** (no positional encoding) in decoder-only models (Kazemnejad et al. 2023) and
   hybrid schemes that interleave RoPE and NoPE layers (Llama 4's iRoPE; verify details
   before claiming; if unverifiable, mark unverified or omit).
6. **Length generalization**: why models fail beyond the training length (unseen rotation
   angles in low-frequency dims, attention entropy), the difference between extrapolation and
   interpolation, and a pointer to PI/NTK/YaRN on page 10 (do not derive them here).
7. **Comparison table**: method, where it enters (input vs attention logits), learned or
   fixed, relative or absolute, extrapolation behavior, used by.

## Required worked numbers
- For \(d_h = 128\): \(\theta_0 = 1\) rad/token (wavelength \(2\pi \approx 6.3\) tokens) and
  the slowest pair's wavelength for \(b = 10^4\) and \(b = 5 \times 10^5\), computed explicitly
  (\(\lambda_{63} = 2\pi b^{126/128}\)); compare with context lengths 8K and 128K.
- ALiBi slopes for 8 heads: \(2^{-1}, 2^{-2}, \dots, 2^{-8}\) (verify).

## Required interview questions (12–16 total; include these)
- Why does a transformer need positional information? Does a causal decoder strictly need it?
- Derive RoPE's relative-position property.
- Why apply RoPE to queries and keys but not values?
- What does the RoPE base control? Why did Llama 3 raise it to 500,000?
- Interleaved vs rotate_half RoPE: are they equivalent? What breaks if you mix them up?
- RoPE vs ALiBi: how do they differ, and which extrapolates better in practice?
- Why is RoPE incompatible with MLA's weight absorption? (short answer, link 07)
