# Brief 10 · Long context: extension & evaluation

- File: `pages/10-long-context.html` · Part II · Modern architecture · P0 (reader's research area) · ~60 min · body 6,000–8,000 words
- Prereqs: 05, 06, 07, 09. Drill module name: `rope_scaling`

## Purpose
How models get from a 4K–8K pretraining context to 128K–1M tokens, and how to tell whether they
actually use it. This is the reader's research area, so be precise about the RoPE-scaling math,
the training recipes of named models, and the evaluation pitfalls; end with open problems.

## Must cover
1. **Why not train long from the start**: attention FLOPs and activation memory grow with T;
   most data is short; the standard recipe is short pretraining then a long-context
   continued-pretraining stage. Chinese: 长上下文, 上下文窗口, 外推, 插值.
2. **Why naive extrapolation fails** with RoPE: low-frequency dimensions see rotation angles
   never seen in training; attention entropy/scale shifts with length.
3. **RoPE scaling methods** with exact formulas:
   - Position Interpolation (Chen et al. 2023): scale positions by \(L_{train}/L_{target}\);
     why it works with a short fine-tune; its cost (crowds high-frequency dims).
   - "NTK-aware" scaling: raise the base so low frequencies interpolate while high
     frequencies are nearly unchanged (give the base-change formula \(b' = b \cdot
     s^{d/(d-2)}\); verify against the YaRN paper's presentation); dynamic NTK.
   - **YaRN** (Peng et al. 2023): per-dimension treatment by wavelength relative to the
     context (ramp between α and β), plus the attention temperature \(\sqrt{1/t} = 0.1 \ln s +
     1\); verify all constants.
   - Base-frequency increase during continued pretraining (ABF, Xiong et al. 2023); Llama 3's
     base of 500,000 and Llama 3.1's rope_scaling (factor 8, low/high frequency factors;
     verify from config and explain what the "llama3" rope type does).
   - LongRoPE (non-uniform, searched rescaling; 2M context claims).
   - DeepSeek-V3: YaRN with s = 40, α = 1, β = 32, applied only to the decoupled key
     \(k^R\) (link 07); two 1,000-step stages 4K → 32K → 128K (verified in the V3 report §4.3).
4. **Training recipes** (verify each): Llama 3 (progressive stages up to 128K and the tokens
   spent; how they judged readiness), DeepSeek-V3 (above), Qwen2.5 / Qwen2.5-1M (progressive
   extension, YaRN + dual chunk attention at inference), and any 2025–2026 1M-context open
   model with a documented recipe. Data engineering (Fu et al. 2024): upsample long documents
   *per domain* keeping the domain mixture; quantity needed (hundreds of millions to billions
   of tokens). Sequence packing and document masking at long lengths.
5. **Systems for long sequences** (brief, link to 15): context/sequence parallelism, ring
   attention; activation checkpointing; chunked prefill at inference; KV memory (link 07).
6. **Evaluation**: perplexity on long documents (and why it is a weak signal); needle in a
   haystack (and why saturating it means little); RULER (multi-needle, variable tracking,
   aggregation; effective context length vs claimed); "lost in the middle"; LongBench v2,
   HELMET, and any newer standard (verify). What a good long-context eval suite for a research
   paper looks like.
7. **Failure modes and open problems**: effective context far below the window, recency bias,
   degradation of short-context quality after extension, reasoning over long inputs vs
   retrieval, length generalization beyond training length; connect to architectures on page 09.

- Why does DeepSeek apply YaRN only to the decoupled RoPE key?
