# Brief 11 · Training stability & modern tweaks

- File: `pages/11-stability-tweaks.html` · Part II · Modern architecture · P1 · ~50 min · body 4,500–6,500 words
- Prereqs: 03, 04, 05. Drill module name: `stability`

## Purpose
The small architectural changes that separate a 2019 transformer from a 2025 one, almost all
motivated by training stability at scale or by attention pathologies. Interviewers ask "your
loss spiked at step 40K, what do you do?" and "why QK-norm?". Explain each tweak with its
failure mode, mechanism, evidence and adopters.

## Must cover
1. **Loss spikes and divergence**: what they look like; PaLM's response (restart from an
   earlier checkpoint and skip data batches) and OPT's logbook-style experience; the diagnostic
   list (data, LR, attention logits, output logits, Adam state, precision). Chinese: 训练稳定性,
   损失尖峰.
2. **Small-scale proxies** (Wortsman et al. 2023): attention-logit growth and output-logit
   divergence reproduced at small scale with high LR; QK-layernorm and z-loss as fixes; LR
   sensitivity as the measurement.
3. **QK-norm**: normalize queries and keys (per head) before the dot product; ViT-22B origin;
   adopters (OLMo 2, Gemma 3, Qwen3; verify); interaction with RoPE (norm before rotation) and
   with MLA (does not apply to absorbed latents directly; say what DeepSeek does instead if
   verifiable, otherwise omit).
4. **z-loss**: \(\lambda \log^2 Z\) on the output softmax normalizer (PaLM, λ = 1e-4; verify);
   router z-loss for MoE (link 08).
5. **Logit soft-capping**: \(c \tanh(z/c)\) on attention and final logits (Gemma 2 values;
   verify) and why Gemma 3 replaced attention soft-capping with QK-norm (verify).
6. **Norm placement**: pre-norm recap (link 04); sandwich/peri-norm (Gemma 2/3 pre+post);
   OLMo 2's reordering (norm on sublayer outputs inside the residual branch; verify the exact
   formula) plus removing weight decay on embeddings; DeepNorm residual scaling; embedding
   scaling by \(\sqrt{d}\) (Gemma).
7. **Hyper-connections** and DeepSeek's manifold-constrained hyper-connections (mHC): widen the
   residual stream into several streams with learnable mixing; what mHC constrains and why
   (verify the paper and its claims before writing; if the paper cannot be found, cover
   hyper-connections only).
8. **Attention sinks and gating**: why softmax forces attention mass somewhere; sink tokens;
   learned sink logits (gpt-oss); **gated attention** (Qwen team 2025: an elementwise
   sigmoid gate after attention output removes massive activations/sinks and improves
   stability; verify ID and claims); "softmax + 1" style ideas briefly.
9. **QK-clip / MuonClip** (Kimi K2): capping per-head max attention logits by rescaling
   \(W^Q, W^K\) during optimization; why Muon made logit explosion worse (link 14).
10. **Multi-token prediction** (Gloeckle et al. 2024; DeepSeek-V3's sequential MTP modules
    with shared embedding/head, loss weight 0.3 → 0.1; used for speculative decoding at
    inference): what the extra objective buys (denser signal, planning) and costs. Verify V3's
    ablation numbers.
11. **A checklist table**: tweak, failure mode addressed, cost, adopters.

## Update (coordinator, DeepSeek-V4)
Manifold-constrained hyper-connections (mHC; Xie et al. 2026) are used in DeepSeek-V4 (n_hc = 4, Sinkhorn-Knopp with 20 iterations to constrain the residual mixing matrix; §2.2 of 2606.19348). V4 also uses per-head RMSNorm on queries and KV entries before attention (a QK-norm analogue) and learned attention sinks. Cover mHC properly (find and verify the Xie et al. 2026 paper).
