# Brief 15 · Memory, precision & parallelism

- File: `pages/15-distributed-training.html` · Part III · Pretraining & systems · P0 · ~80 min · body 7,000–9,000 words
- Prereqs: 04, 08, 14. Drill module name: `parallel`

## Purpose
Research-engineer loops always include "how would you train model X on N GPUs": memory
accounting, precision, and the parallelism toolbox (DP/ZeRO/FSDP, TP, SP, PP, EP, CP). The
reader must be able to size a job on a whiteboard and explain communication costs.

## Must cover
1. **Memory accounting** for training: weights, gradients, optimizer states (mixed-precision
   Adam: 2 + 2 + 12 = 16 bytes/param with fp32 master weights and moments; ZeRO paper's
   accounting), activations (per-layer formula from Korthikanti et al. 2022, \(sbh(34 +
   5as/h)\) bytes; verify and explain terms), temporary buffers, fragmentation. Activation
   checkpointing (full vs selective). Chinese: 混合精度, 数据并行, 张量并行, 流水线并行, 激活重计算.
2. **Numerics**: fp32/fp16/bf16/fp8 (E4M3, E5M2) bit layouts and dynamic range; why bf16 needs
   no loss scaling; fp16 loss scaling; FP8 training: per-tensor delayed scaling (Transformer
   Engine) vs DeepSeek-V3's fine-grained tile/block scaling (1×128 activations, 128×128 weights)
   with higher-precision accumulation (verified in V3 §3.3); MXFP8/MXFP4/NVFP4 microscaling
   briefly (verify).
3. **Collectives and their costs**: all-reduce (ring: \(2(n-1)/n\) × size per device),
   reduce-scatter, all-gather, all-to-all; bandwidth numbers for NVLink vs InfiniBand (cite
   V3's 160 vs 50 GB/s statement, verified in §3.2.2).
4. **Data parallelism** → **ZeRO-1/2/3** and **FSDP**: what each shards, communication volume
   (ZeRO-3 = 1.5× DP), when to use which.
5. **Tensor parallelism** (Megatron-LM): column- then row-parallel MLP, head-parallel attention,
   the two all-reduces per layer forward; why TP stays within a node. **Sequence parallelism**
   (Korthikanti et al.) for norms/dropout activations.
6. **Pipeline parallelism**: GPipe, 1F1B, interleaved 1F1B; bubble fraction \((p-1)/(m+p-1)\)
   (derive); zero-bubble scheduling; DualPipe (brief, link 27).
7. **Expert parallelism** (link 08) and **context parallelism** / ring attention for long
   sequences (link 10).
8. **Putting it together**: 3D/4D/5D parallel layouts; worked plans for (a) Llama-3-8B on 64
   H100s, (b) a 70B dense model on 1,024 GPUs, (c) DeepSeek-V3's actual layout (PP16 × EP64 ×
   ZeRO-1 DP, no TP; verified in V3 §3.2); Llama 3 405B's layout (verify from the paper).
9. **MFU** (model FLOPs utilization) vs HFU: definition, formula with 6N (+ attention term),
   typical values (verify Llama 3's reported MFU range and PaLM's 46.2%).

## Required interview questions (14–16; include these)
- How much memory does it take to train a 7B model with AdamW in mixed precision? Where does it go?
- Explain ZeRO-1/2/3 and their communication costs.
- How does tensor parallelism split an MLP and attention? Why keep it within a node?
- Derive the pipeline bubble fraction. How do you reduce it?
- bf16 vs fp16 vs fp8: why can bf16 skip loss scaling? How does DeepSeek train in FP8?
- Plan the parallelism for training a 70B model on 1,024 H100s with 8K context.
- What is MFU and how do you compute it?
