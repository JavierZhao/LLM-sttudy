# Brief 23 · RLHF in practice

- File: `pages/23-rlhf.html` · Part V · Reinforcement learning · P0 · ~50 min · body 4,500–6,500 words
- Prereqs: 21, 22. Drill module name: `rlhf`

## Purpose
The classic RLHF pipeline as practiced (InstructGPT, Anthropic HH, Llama 2), its engineering
realities, its failure modes, and the move to AI feedback and process supervision.

## Must cover
1. **The InstructGPT pipeline**: SFT → RM → PPO with per-token KL penalty and the pretraining
   mix (PPO-ptx); dataset sizes and model sizes (verify); the headline result (1.3B InstructGPT
   preferred over 175B GPT-3); alignment tax. Chinese: 基于人类反馈的强化学习, 奖励黑客.
2. **Engineering**: four models (policy, reference, reward, value) and their memory; generation
   dominates wall-clock; reward normalization, advantage whitening, value init from RM, adaptive
   KL controller (target KL), EOS handling and truncation penalties (cite the "N implementation
   details" paper).
3. **Anthropic HH** (helpful/harmless tension, online iterated RLHF) and **Llama 2** (separate
   helpfulness and safety RMs, margin term in the RM loss, rejection sampling + PPO iterations,
   Ghost Attention; verify details).
4. **Failure modes**: reward hacking (length, sycophancy, hedging, formatting), mode collapse /
   diversity loss, over-optimization (link 21), annotator disagreement.
5. **RLAIF and Constitutional AI**: critique-revise SFT then RL from AI preference labels
   against a constitution; Bai et al. 2022; RLAIF vs RLHF comparisons (Lee et al. 2023; verify).
6. **Process vs outcome supervision**: PRMs (Lightman et al. 2023, PRM800K; best-of-N with PRM
   vs ORM results), Math-Shepherd automatic process labels; why DeepSeek-R1 reported PRMs as an
   unsuccessful attempt (link 24).
7. **Generative reward models and rubrics** (LLM-as-judge rewards; DeepSeek-V3.2's per-prompt
   rubrics; Kimi K2's self-critique rubric reward; verified in those reports).
