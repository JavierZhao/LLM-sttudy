# Brief 24 · RLVR & reasoning models: GRPO and successors

- File: `pages/24-rlvr-reasoning.html` · Part V · Reinforcement learning · P0 · ~80 min · body 7,000–9,000 words
- Prereqs: 22, 23. Drill module name: `grpo`

## Purpose
Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards is how reasoning models (o1-style, DeepSeek-R1,
Qwen3 thinking, Kimi) are trained, and GRPO plus its fixes dominate 2025–2026 interviews. Cover
the algorithms precisely, the empirical phenomena, the ongoing debates, and practical recipes.
Search for developments after your training data (as of September 2026).

## Must cover
1. **RLVR setup**: rule-based rewards (answer matching with normalization, code unit tests,
   format rewards), why verifiable rewards resist hacking better than learned RMs, and their
   limits (only checkable domains, false positives in checkers). Chinese: 可验证奖励, 思维链,
   测试时扩展.
2. **GRPO** (DeepSeekMath): sample G outputs per prompt; advantage \((r_i - \text{mean})/\text{std}\)
   shared by all tokens of output i; no critic; PPO-style clipped ratio; KL to reference in the
   loss with the k3 estimator; the exact objective with its normalizations (per-sequence 1/|o_i|
   then 1/G; write it exactly as the paper does and verify).
3. **DeepSeek-R1-Zero and R1** (verify every number): pure RL from V3-Base with accuracy and
   format rewards; rising response length and emergent reflection ("aha moment"); problems
   (readability, language mixing); the R1 pipeline (cold-start long-CoT SFT → reasoning RL with
   a language-consistency reward → rejection sampling ~600K reasoning + ~200K non-reasoning
   SFT → RL for all scenarios); distillation to Qwen/Llama and the finding that distillation beats
   RL at small scale; unsuccessful attempts (PRM, MCTS) and why.
4. **Fixes and successors** (one subsection each, with the precise change and its motivation):
   - **DAPO**: clip-higher (decoupled ε_low/ε_high to fight entropy collapse), dynamic sampling
     (drop groups with all-correct or all-wrong answers since their advantages are zero),
     token-level loss aggregation (long responses not down-weighted), overlong reward shaping;
     reported result (verify).
   - **Dr. GRPO**: the length bias from 1/|o_i| and the difficulty bias from std normalization;
     remove both.
   - **GSPO** (Qwen): sequence-level importance ratio (length-normalized) and sequence-level
     clipping; motivation from MoE instability (routing changes between old and new policy).
   - **CISPO** (MiniMax-M1): clip the importance-sampling weight (stop-gradient) instead of the
     update, so all tokens keep gradients.
   - **RLOO** and **REINFORCE++**: critic-free baselines without group std normalization.
5. **Dynamics**: entropy collapse and its predictors (verify the "entropy mechanism" paper's
   covariance result), response-length growth, overthinking and length penalties (V3.2 uses a
   length penalty; verified), difficulty filtering and curricula (pass-rate filtering).
6. **Does RL add capability?** Yue et al. 2025: RL improves pass@1 but base models match or
   beat RL models at large k, so RL mostly sharpens; counter-evidence (e.g. prolonged RL /
   ProRL; verify); spurious-reward results on Qwen2.5-Math (verify the paper and scope
   carefully). Present as an open debate.
7. **Test-time scaling**: long CoT, parallel sampling + voting, sequential revision; budget
   forcing (s1); thinking budgets and hybrid thinking modes (Qwen3, DeepSeek-V3.1; verify);
   scaling RL compute (V3.2: >10% of pretraining cost; verified).
8. **A practical recipe** for a small RLVR experiment (model, data, reward, G, lr, KL or no KL,
   max length, eval) with the reasoning behind each choice; this is useful for the reader.

## Required interview questions (14–16; include these)
- Write the GRPO objective. How does it differ from PPO?
- Why is there no value model in GRPO, and what replaces it?
- What biases does GRPO's normalization introduce, and how do Dr. GRPO and DAPO fix them?
- What happened in DeepSeek-R1-Zero, and why did R1 need a cold start?
- Why do groups where all samples are correct or all wrong contribute nothing?
- What is entropy collapse and how do you prevent it?
- Does RL teach models new reasoning abilities? Argue both sides with evidence.
- Design an RLVR run to improve a 7B model's math accuracy. What would you monitor?
