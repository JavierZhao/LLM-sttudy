## Purpose
DeepSeek is the reader's top-priority model family. This page tells the 2023–mid-2024 story:
each paper's key idea, *why* DeepSeek made each choice (often compute/hardware efficiency), and
the evidence, so the reader can discuss DeepSeek's research style and lineage fluently in an
interview. Earlier pages teach the mechanisms; here, synthesize and link back rather than
re-derive.

## Must cover (verify every number from the papers)
1. **DeepSeek LLM** (Jan 2024, 7B/67B, 2T tokens): scaling laws for hyperparameters (optimal
   batch size and LR as power laws of compute; give the fitted formulas), using non-embedding
   FLOPs/token M instead of N for model scale, the finding that data quality shifts the optimal
   model/data allocation, multi-step LR schedule and why (continual training), architecture
   choices (depth vs width for 67B, GQA for 67B; verify), alignment (SFT + DPO).
2. **DeepSeekMoE** (Jan 2024): fine-grained expert segmentation and shared-expert isolation
   (link 08 for mechanics), the scaled experiments (2B, 16B, 145B) and comparisons with GShard
   and dense models.
3. **DeepSeek-Coder** briefly (repository-level data, FIM) and **DeepSeekMath** (Feb 2024): the
   DeepSeekMath corpus (iterative fastText-based mining of ~120B math tokens from Common Crawl;
   verify), GRPO's introduction (link 24), and the "unified paradigm" view that expresses SFT,
   RFT, DPO, PPO and GRPO as (data source, reward function, gradient coefficient).
4. **DeepSeek-V2** (May 2024): 236B total / 21B activated, 8.1T tokens, 128K context via YaRN;
   MLA (link 07), DeepSeekMoE with 2 shared + 160 routed experts, 6 active, device-limited
   routing (M = 3), three balance losses and token dropping (link 08); training (HAI-LLM,
   16-way zero-bubble PP, 8-way EP, ZeRO-1, no TP; verified in §3.1.3); alignment (1.5M SFT
   sessions; GRPO RL in two stages, reasoning then human preference; online RL beats offline);
   efficiency claims vs DeepSeek 67B (42.5% training cost, 93.3% KV cache, 5.76× throughput)
   with the caveats from page 07; DeepSeek-V2-Lite (15.7B/2.4B) as a research artifact.
5. **DeepSeek-V2.5 / Coder-V2** in one paragraph (verify what they changed).
6. **The DeepSeek research style**: co-design with hardware constraints (H800 interconnect),
   efficiency-first choices, open weights and detailed reports, willingness to publish negative
   results. Keep it factual and sourced.
7. **Timeline figure/table** from DeepSeek LLM to V2.5 with dates, params, tokens, key idea.
