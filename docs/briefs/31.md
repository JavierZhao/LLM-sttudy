# Brief 31 · Mistral, Gemma, gpt-oss

## Purpose
Three families that each popularized specific ideas: Mistral (sliding-window attention, open
MoE), Gemma (local/global attention, soft-capping → QK-norm, distillation-based pretraining),
and OpenAI's gpt-oss (attention sinks, banded attention, MXFP4 MoE, harmony format). Search for
releases after your training data (as of September 2026) and include only verified ones.

## Must cover (verify every number)
1. **Mistral 7B**: GQA (8 KV heads), sliding window 4,096 with rolling buffer cache, receptive
   field argument, chunked prefill. **Mixtral 8x7B / 8x22B**: 8 experts top-2, total vs active
   params, routing analysis. Later Mistral releases with public technical detail (e.g. Magistral
   RL report; Mistral Small/Medium/Large 3; verify architecture claims). Chinese: 滑动窗口注意力,
   注意力汇聚.
2. **Gemma 1**: MQA for 2B, head dim 256, GeGLU, 256K vocab, embedding scaling (verified: 2B uses
   MQA; embedding params 524,550,144 for 2B).
3. **Gemma 2**: alternating local (4,096) / global (8,192) layers, logit soft-capping (attention
   and final), pre + post norms, GQA, knowledge distillation from a larger teacher to train the
   small models on many more effective tokens (verify ratios and the distillation claim).
4. **Gemma 3**: 5:1 local:global with a 1,024-token window, QK-norm replacing soft-capping, RoPE
   base 1M for global and 10K for local layers, 128K context, KV-cache savings analysis,
   multimodal SigLIP encoder (one line), distillation. Gemma 3n briefly if verified.
5. **gpt-oss** (Aug 2025; model card): gpt-oss-120b and -20b total/active params, MoE (experts,
   top-4), alternating dense and banded (128-token) attention, GQA, learned attention sinks
   (per-head logit in the softmax denominator), YaRN to 131K, MXFP4 weights for MoE layers so
   120b fits on one 80GB GPU, o200k_harmony tokenizer and the harmony chat format, reasoning
   effort levels, training similar to o-series (as stated). Verify every number in the card.
6. **Comparison table**: attention pattern, KV heads, context, norm/stability tricks, MoE config,
   tokenizer, license.

## Required interview questions (10–12; include these)

- Why did Gemma 3 switch from soft-capping to QK-norm?
