## Purpose

Kimi (Muon at 1T scale, MuonClip, agentic RL, linear attention), GLM (deep-narrow MoE, slime RL),
MiniMax (lightning-attention hybrids, CISPO, and the return to full attention), OLMo (fully open
recipes and stability), NVIDIA Nemotron (Mamba hybrids, FP8). Search for releases after your
training data (as of September 2026) and include only verified ones.
