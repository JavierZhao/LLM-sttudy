# Brief 34 · Back-of-envelope calculations

- File: `pages/34-back-of-envelope.html` · Part VII · Interview toolkit · P0 · ~45 min · body 4,000–5,500 words plus drills
- Prereqs: 04, 06, 07, 08, 13, 15. Drill module name: `napkin`

## Purpose
A single page with every formula an interviewer expects you to use without notes, the
hardware constants to memorize, and 25+ worked drills with hidden answers, in increasing
difficulty. Consistency with earlier pages is essential: reuse their numbers.

## Must cover
1. **Constants to memorize** (verify from datasheets): H100/H800 SXM bf16 dense and FP8 FLOP/s,
   HBM capacity and bandwidth, NVLink and InfiniBand bandwidths, A100 for comparison, and newer
   GPUs (B200/GB200) if verified; bytes per dtype.
2. **Formulas**: parameter counts (dense with GQA, MLA, MoE total vs active); forward FLOPs
   per token 2N + attention term; training 6ND; training time = C / (n_gpu × peak × MFU); memory
   for training (16 bytes/param + activations) and inference (weights + KV); KV bytes per token
   for each attention type; decode time lower bound (bytes read / bandwidth) and prefill time
   (FLOPs / (peak × MFU)); ring all-reduce time for gradients; pipeline bubble; MoE all-to-all
   bytes per token; Chinchilla optimum; cost in dollars from GPU-hours.
3. **Worked drills** (25+), each as a `details.qa` with a full solution showing arithmetic,
   grouped: parameters and FLOPs; memory; training time and cost; inference latency/throughput;
   KV cache; communication; scaling laws; MoE. Include at least: Llama-3-8B params; DeepSeek-V3
   activated params; 70B training memory; 405B training time on 16K GPUs at 40% MFU vs the paper;
   8B decode tokens/s at batch 1 and 64; KV cache for 32 users × 32K on a 70B GQA model;
   gradient all-reduce time for 8B params over 8 GPUs with NVLink; compute-optimal model at 1e23
   FLOPs; cost of 1T tokens of RL rollouts at a given throughput.
4. **Sanity-check habits**: orders of magnitude, units, what dominates, stating assumptions out loud.
