# Brief 36 · Question bank & mock scenarios

- File: `pages/36-question-bank.html` · Part VII · Interview toolkit · P0 · ~120 min · generated + written
- Prereqs: all pages. No new drill (the page links to all drills). Use drill module name: none;
  the QA script's exercises section should link to page 35.

## Purpose
All interview questions in one place for spaced practice, plus open-ended mock interview
scenarios of the kind used in research-scientist and research-engineer loops.

## Build (two parts)
1. **Aggregated bank (mechanical, Haiku)**: extract every `details.qa` from pages 01–35 with a
   script (keep tier, source page link, question, answer HTML). Group by part and topic, keep the
   tier filter, and add a "random 10" practice button (vanilla JS, no storage required; if
   using localStorage for "seen" marks, wrap in try/catch).
2. **Mock scenarios (written, Sonnet/Opus)**: 12–15 scenarios, each with the prompt, what a
   strong answer covers (bullet rubric), common weak answers, and follow-up questions. Mix:
   - Research discussion: "Design an efficient 1M-context model", "Propose and evaluate an
     alternative to MLA", "Is RL teaching reasoning or sharpening it? Design an experiment."
   - Debugging: "Loss spike at step 40K", "MoE experts collapse", "RL reward rises but eval falls",
     "Long-context eval regresses after extension".
   - Systems: "Plan training of a 70B model on 2K GPUs", "Cut inference cost of a 671B MoE by 2×".
   - Paper deep dive: "Walk me through DeepSeek-V3 / R1 and critique one design choice".
   - Behavioral-technical: "Tell me about a time an experiment result surprised you" framed for
     the reader's physics background (tie jet-tagging experience to LLM research skills).

## Do not
Duplicate answers inconsistently: the bank must be generated from the pages so fixes propagate.
