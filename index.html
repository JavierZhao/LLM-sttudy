LLM Interview Prep

A ground-up course on how frontier language models are built: architecture, pretraining, finetuning and reinforcement learning. It is written for interviews at frontier labs and assumes solid ML fundamentals but no prior LLM experience.

How to use this course

  • Go in order. Each page builds on the previous ones, and the prerequisites are listed in its header. Parts I and II are the base everything else rests on.
  • P0 pages must be known cold. P1 pages matter but come up less often. P2 pages are reference material. The P0 only filter below is the shortest serious path, about 20 hours.
  • Answer each interview question aloud before you open the answer. Reading an answer and producing it under pressure are different skills.
  • Do the drills by hand. Every page has runnable exercises in exercises/ with tests. Coding rounds routinely ask for attention, RoPE, KV-cache decoding, sampling, or a DPO/GRPO loss from scratch.
  • DeepSeek is the running example. MLA, DeepSeekMoE and GRPO are taught where their concepts first appear, and pages 26 to 28 put the whole lineage together.
On accuracy

These pages were drafted with AI help and fact-checked against the primary papers, but errors are still possible. Claims that could not be verified are marked like this. For the derivations you are most likely to be grilled on (RoPE, MLA, DPO, PPO/GRPO, scaling laws, memory math), treat the linked papers as the ground truth.

What every page contains

  1. What interviewers probe: the angles you will be tested on.
  2. The body: intuition, then math, then minimal PyTorch, then a diagram, then worked numbers on two reference models (Llama-3-8B and DeepSeek-V3).
  3. Design decision cards: problem, idea, why it works, evidence, cost, who uses it.
  4. Common misconceptions.
  5. Interview questions in three tiers (warm-up, core, deep-dive), with answers collapsed.
  6. Hands-on drills, primary sources, and a one-screen cheat sheet.

Running the exercises

pytest exercises --solutions                 # check the reference solutions instead
