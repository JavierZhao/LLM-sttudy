Question bank & mock scenarios

P0 ~120 min Prereqs: all pages; drills on 35 Coding drills

Every interview question from the finished pages in one place, filterable by part and tier, with a random practice set, plus open-ended mock scenarios of the kind used in research-scientist and research-engineer loops.

How to use this page
  • Answer aloud before you open an answer. Recognizing a correct answer is much easier than producing one under time pressure, and only the second is tested.
  • Practice in short random sets. "Random 10" draws from whatever the filters currently allow, so you can drill one part, one tier, or everything.
  • Follow the source link. Each answer links to the page it comes from; if an answer surprises you, reread that section rather than memorizing the answer.
  • The bank is generated from the pages. It is rebuilt from the source pages on every site build, so a correction to a page reaches this bank automatically.

After this page you can

  • Answer the core questions of every part of the course without notes, at the depth a frontier-lab interviewer expects.
  • Run a mock interview on yourself or with a partner: pick a scenario, answer for 20 to 40 minutes, and grade against the rubric.
  • Find your weak parts quickly by filtering the bank and sampling random sets.

The question bank

Questions are grouped by part and keep their tier: warm-up (definitions and one-step reasoning), core (the questions most loops ask), and deep-dive (derivations, design trade-offs, and follow-ups). Answers load when you open them.
